HUONG DAN CAU HINH CAC CHUC NANG MOI
====================================

1. Tao su kien canh bao nguoi la tren Blynk
--------------------------------------------
- Mo Blynk Console, chon Template dang dung cho khoa cua.
- Vao Events, tao mot Event moi.
- Event code phai ghi dung: stranger_alert
- Bat Notifications/Push notification cho Event nay.

Neu khong tao Event code stranger_alert thi ESP32 van phat hien va luu
nhat ky nguoi la, nhung dien thoai se khong hien thong bao day.


2. Tao them cac Datastream tren Blynk
-------------------------------------
- V9:  String  - Nhat ky truy cap moi nhat.
- V10: Integer - Thoi gian xac thuc, don vi ms, khoang 0 den 5000.
- V11: Integer - So the RFID da dang ky, khoang 0 den 120.
- V12: Integer - Thoi gian phat lenh canh bao, don vi ms, khoang 0 den 10000.

Tren giao dien dien thoai co the dung Label/Value Display cho V9-V12.
Datastream V5 dung de chon ID the can xoa, can doi khoang gia tri thanh
0 den 119.


3. Thu tu nap chuong trinh
-------------------------
- Nap chuong trinh ESP32-S3 truoc.
- Sau do nap chuong trinh STM32.

Giao thuc UART moi gui ID the va thoi gian xac thuc tu STM32 sang ESP32.
ESP32 van giu xu ly ky tu 'a' de tuong thich voi ban STM32 cu.


4. Ket qua can quan sat khi kiem thu
-----------------------------------
- The hop le: Serial hien [AUTH] RFID, ID, thoi gian ms va PASS/FAIL.
- Khuon mat hop le: Serial hien [AUTH] FACE, ID, thoi gian ms va PASS/FAIL.
- Nguoi la: dien thoai nhan thong bao "CANH BAO: Phat hien nguoi la...".
- Serial hien [ALERT] va thoi gian phat lenh canh bao.
- V9 hien nhat ky moi nhat kem ngay gio, phuong thuc, ket qua va thoi gian.
- Nhat ky 100 lan truy cap gan nhat duoc luu trong NVS cua ESP32-S3.
- STM32 quan ly toi da 120 the RFID, ID tu 0 den 119.

PASS chi xuat hien khi thoi gian xac thuc nho hon 1000 ms hoac thoi gian
phat lenh canh bao nho hon 5000 ms. Can do tren mo hinh that de lay so lieu
dua vao bao cao; toc do mang va may chu Blynk co the anh huong thoi gian
thong bao thuc su xuat hien tren dien thoai.
